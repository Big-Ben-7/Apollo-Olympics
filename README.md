# Apollo Olympics
 A 3D space racing video game with customizable ships, special abilities, asteroid hazards, collectibles, and split-screen local multiplayer support. Players roll to evade and confuse targeting drones. A chunk-based object pooling technique is implemented for efficiency with the open world. Top 10 Finalist at the 2025 TSA National Conference, 1st Place at the 2025 PA TSA State Conference and Region 8 Conference.
